# Cat Care

_General guidance, not veterinary advice. Check with your vet for your pet's specific needs._

## Feeding
Adult cats usually eat two measured meals a day. Wet food adds helpful hydration.

## Play
Cats benefit from 10 to 20 minutes of active play, once or twice a day, using wand toys or chasing games. This helps prevent boredom and weight gain.

## Litter box
Scoop at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly. Provide one box per cat plus one extra.

## Grooming
Short-haired cats need brushing about once a week. Long-haired cats need brushing every day or two to prevent mats.
