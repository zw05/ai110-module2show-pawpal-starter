# Medication

_General guidance, not veterinary advice. Check with your vet for your pet's specific needs._

## Giving medication
Give medication at the same times each day, and follow the vet's dose and duration exactly. Do not stop early, even if your pet seems better.

## Missed doses
If you miss a dose, check the label or call your vet. Do not double up on the next dose unless instructed.

## Safety
Never give human medication to pets without a vet's approval. Many common ones, such as ibuprofen and acetaminophen, are toxic. Contact a vet right away if your pet shows a bad reaction.
