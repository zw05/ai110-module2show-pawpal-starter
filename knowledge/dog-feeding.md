# Dog Feeding

_General guidance, not veterinary advice. Check with your vet for your pet's specific needs._

## Meal schedule
Adult dogs do best with two meals a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies under 6 months usually need three or four small meals.

## Portions
Follow the food label as a starting point and adjust to keep your dog lean. You should be able to feel, but not see, the ribs.

## Water
Fresh water should always be available. Rinse and refill the bowl daily.
