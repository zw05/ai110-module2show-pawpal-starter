# Senior Pets

_General guidance, not veterinary advice. Check with your vet for your pet's specific needs._

## When is a pet senior
Dogs are generally considered senior from about 7 years old (earlier for giant breeds). Cats are considered senior from about 10 years old.

## Exercise for senior dogs
Senior dogs still need daily walks, but shorter and gentler. Try two or three 15 to 20 minute walks a day instead of one long one, and avoid hard running or jumping.

## Care changes
Senior pets often need more frequent vet checkups, around every 6 months, softer bedding, easy access to food and water, and a diet suited to their age.
