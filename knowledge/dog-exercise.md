# Dog Exercise

_General guidance, not veterinary advice. Check with your vet for your pet's specific needs._

## Daily walks
Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of exercise per day, usually split into two or more walks. High-energy breeds such as retrievers, collies and terriers may need up to two hours.

## Puppies
Puppies need short, frequent play sessions. A common rule is about 5 minutes of structured exercise per month of age, up to twice a day. Avoid long runs while their joints are developing.

## Mental stimulation
Puzzle toys, training sessions and sniff walks tire a dog out as much as physical exercise. Aim for 10 to 15 minutes of enrichment daily.
