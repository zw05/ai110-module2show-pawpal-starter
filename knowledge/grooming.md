# Grooming

_General guidance, not veterinary advice. Check with your vet for your pet's specific needs._

## Brushing
Short-coated dogs need brushing about weekly. Long or thick coats need brushing several times a week to prevent mats.

## Bathing
Most dogs need a bath every 4 to 8 weeks, or when dirty. Cats rarely need baths.

## Nails and teeth
Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks. Brush teeth several times a week, ideally daily, with pet-safe toothpaste.
